## Why

`add-console-dock` (archived `c0b4aad`) put the sighting log in the dock's Log tab but mounts it only while the tab is shown, as the floating log was, so a collapse discards the log's state, opening it is still a read, the dock has no Escape key, and the filed drift `docs/handoffs/drift-sighting-log-not-reread-after-sweep-settles.md` stays masked. This is link 2b-i-b of `docs/plans/system-hud/chain.md`: the content-lifetime half of link 2b that RULING 15 of `docs/handoffs/add-console-dock-rulings.md` split off, now authored against the live specs. Its rulings are `docs/handoffs/keep-console-tab-content-mounted-rulings.md` (16) and its analysis is `docs/handoffs/keep-console-tab-content-mounted-analysis.md`.

## What Changes

- ADD to `system-console` four requirements, each naming the owner of anything it touches in another capability and restating none of it:
  - *A Tab's Content Stays Mounted While It Is Not Shown*: collapsing and re-expanding the dock discards neither the log's loaded pages, nor the selected row and its statement, nor the state of the log's read; the hidden content is out of sight, out of the tab order and out of assistive technology, and a row's highlight is not presented while its tab is not shown.
  - *Showing or Hiding the Log Tab Issues No Read*: showing, hiding and re-showing the Log tab issue no request, and the log's first read does not wait for an expansion.
  - *Escape Collapses the Expanded Dock*: Escape collapses an expanded dock from anywhere in the view, unless the fleet cell's open menu takes the key (what Escape does to the dock while that menu is open with keyboard focus elsewhere is stated nowhere); one press does one thing; a collapse by Escape or by the collapse control moves no keyboard focus that is outside the tab bar and the panel; Escape changes no selection, no acting fleet and issues no request.
  - *Activating the Dock Changes No Space-View Selection*, mirroring the strip's own requirement.
- MODIFY one `system-console` requirement, *The Log Tab Is the Sighting Log's Home on This View*: its gate sentence is falsified by the mount latch (the log waits for the interior read the view opened with, whether a first load or the re-read of an interior the view already held, where the view opened with one, and then stays), and two carried scenarios' GIVENs with it (the first, and the report link's on a collapsed dock). Ten live scenario headings are carried verbatim, three new scenarios cover the warm-cache case. The three "first load" headings keep their cold-cache bodies.
- Build, frontend only: the Log tab's content is always present in the view and hidden while collapsed, behind a latch that opens once; the settle re-read reads the unfiltered pile at both settle exits (closing the drift record, no delta on `sighting-log`: a live scenario there becomes true); the dock-wide Escape handling, routed through the shell's existing collapse path and its focus rule.
- Delete what this replaces: the view's mount-on-show expression and its `!interior.isLoading` gate for the log. No flag, no shim, and nothing is kept beside the new behaviour.
- Migrate the e2e specs whose preconditions move when the log reads at view open (`scan-lane-transit-fizzle`, `scan-journey`), note the two that only need a comment (`vitals-strip-journey`, `console-dock-journey`), re-read `SystemSpaceView.console-dock.test.tsx` and run the `SystemSpaceView*` family against the second unmocked sightings read each now carries.
- Not a delta: `sighting-log` and `system-space-view` take none (RULING 1). *The log refetches after a sweep settles* (`sighting-log`) becomes true by a build task and view-level tests over the real hook; the drift record is closed at the gate.

### Costs said out loud

- **One more request at every view open (analysis § 11 risk 1).** The log reads once the interior read the view opened with has finished, for a surface that may never be shown.
- **"The log reads when it mounts" is not literally true on a fresh cache entry (RULING 4).** The app-wide 60 s stale time means a mount inside the window issues no request, on the warm arm (a return to an interior the view already held) as on the cold one. The first-read scenario is conditioned on a view opened by loading the document, the warm-arm scenario on the client holding no pile answer fresher than it keeps a read, and the literal gap in `sighting-log` is filed, not fixed.
- **A record minted elsewhere is not listed on re-expansion (predecessor RULING 8's freshness cost).** It reaches the log on a settled sweep's re-read or on explicit refresh. Re-expansion is deliberately not a read, so a hand-opened log is no longer fresher than a mounted one.
- **Reads outside the three named triggers exist and are not specified away (RULING 5).** An always-subscribed log also refetches on network reconnect, and the settle watcher's retry loop re-reads the pile every attempt while a past-ETA sweep stays unsettled. This capability states only that showing and hiding issue no request.
- **Escape and an open menu that holds no focus (RULING 7).** The Escape requirement promises the dock stays expanded only when the open menu takes the key (focus on the chevron or on one of its entries). In the state the filed drift `drift-fleet-menu-ignores-escape-when-unfocused.md` describes (an empty menu opened by pointer in a browser that does not focus a clicked button, or focus that left an open menu), Escape collapses the dock and leaves the menu open. The drift stays out of this change; its evidence is narrowed at the gate.
- **The panel's scroll position across a collapse is unspecified (RULING 8)**; the journey logs it. A hidden, highlighted row is "not presented" (RULING 10) with no `sighting-log` delta.
- **A failed load-more or a failed re-read over listed rows (RULING 12)** is `sighting-log`'s to specify and is unspecified there; the failed-while-collapsed scenario is arranged on the first read only, and the gap is filed.
- **The highlighted row is not brought into view (RULING 13).** At 1280x720 the panel shows one to two rows, so a highlighted row can lie beyond the visible part. The scroll is shed to `docs/handoffs/followup-log-highlighted-row-into-view.md`, for 2b-ii's author.
- **A permanently present log re-renders every loaded row whenever the view renders (at least once a second) under `display: none`.** Unmeasured, and no suite would notice it: `SystemSpaceView.render-loop` counts commits, not cost. The remedy if it is ever measured is in design.md Risks.
- **A failure of the log's read is silent while the dock is collapsed.** The `sightings-unavailable` card is the ghost feed's; a failure of the log's own unfiltered read shows nowhere until the dock is expanded, where the failed state and its Retry are waiting. With the first read now issued at view open, such a failure is more likely to be sitting there when the player first looks. A collapsed-state failure marker is out of scope.
- **Refresh is the remedy for the freshness cost and gives no feedback of its own over listed rows.** While the re-read is in flight the listed rows show unchanged with no in-progress indication, and a refresh that finds nothing new changes nothing visible. That is `sighting-log`'s to specify and is not changed here.
- **The report's link can open a log whose settle re-read is still in flight (analysis § 5.1 row 10).** The link issues no request (the re-read was issued at the settle), so for one round trip the log lists the rows it held before, without the sweep's new row and with no in-progress indication; the row appears when the re-read answers, and a failed re-read shows the log's failed state in place of the rows (RULING 12).
- **Escape from a text field or a select (RULING 6)** collapses the dock and keeps the field's value; link 2b-iv, which moves those inputs into the dock, should read that row.

### Out of scope, each deliberate

- Tab switching, arrow keys between tabs, "Log tab activatable by Enter or Space" and the Scan tab: link 2b-ii.
- The second-press Escape rule (clear the selection): link 4, which MODIFIES *Escape Collapses the Expanded Dock*.
- The fleet menu's unfocused-Escape drift; the ghost layer's warm-cache mount gate; the settle watcher's disarm race; the join-in-flight first read; the first read inside the interior's stale window (filed at the gate, not fixed).
- A tab count (`Log · N`), a resize control, persisted dock state, camera reframing under the expanded dock, a collapsed-state failure marker for the log's read, and the row-into-view scroll.
- Any backend field, endpoint or query; the galaxy view; a mobile layout; any palette or type change; a resizable dock; React `<Activity>` and `experimental.routerBFCache`.
- Not absorbed: `followup-explain-withheld-burn-command.md`, `drift-commit-response-carries-no-delta-v.md`, `defect-fleet-can-rest-inside-star-clearance.md`, `drift-strip-cells-retain-figures-after-failed-fleets-read.md`.
