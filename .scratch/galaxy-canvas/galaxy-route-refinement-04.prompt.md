Use case: ui-mockup
Create one high-resolution four-panel 2 by 2 visual design review sheet for Hexaverse galaxy-map route styling. This is a realistic polished game-canvas concept, not a diagram. Input image 1 is the LOCKED accepted background: dark navy space, sweeping diagonal teal/amber galactic dust band with dark rifts, fine distant starfield and faint perspective hex grid. Input image 2 is a comparison sheet: use ONLY TOP PANEL A STELLAR as star/lane art reference. Preserve its small dimensional radiant stellar spheres, subtle mottled emissive surfaces, spectral blue-white/gold/red character, compact coronas and fine silver-blue lane filaments. Do not use B's technical bezels or C's broad light ribbons.
Four equally sized wide panels. Left column WIDE ZOOM, right column CLOSE ZOOM. Top row PROPOSED ROUTE, bottom row ACTIVE ROUTE. Clear small external labels: 'PROPOSED · WIDE', 'PROPOSED · CLOSE', 'ACTIVE · WIDE', 'ACTIVE · CLOSE'. Use identical base galaxy and positions at a given zoom in top and bottom so route state alone changes. Right column magnifies a middle stretch of same map and route; it is not a different galaxy. No side HUD, browser chrome, buttons or extra panels. Let map canvas occupy each whole panel.
Show one illustrative route spanning 12 lane hops through 13 system nodes, winding naturally from left lower side through branching network across dust band toward right upper destination. Route follows actual thin lane links between stars, no free floating shortcut spline. Include unselected lane branches and isolated stars so route hierarchy can be judged amid map detail. Ordinary lanes are quiet thin silver-blue filaments. Ordinary background stars are many times smaller/fainter than interactive system nodes. Hex edges remain subordinate. Route should be bright CYAN, visibly stronger than ordinary lanes without obscuring star surfaces.
PROPOSED row: route overlay made of clean moderately spaced cyan DASHES on each chosen lane, clearly visible gaps at both zooms, sparse small cyan directional chevrons pointing forward along the route. Origin has a small open ring. Destination has a distinctive cyan double ring and small diamond pointer, with tiny label 'Destination'. No fleet-progress marker or completed legs in proposed state. Intermediate stars stay natural and do not all get rings, labels or numbers. All 12 legs proposed dashed.
ACTIVE row: SAME route, first four legs already completed: subdued desaturated slate-blue filaments, low but traceable contrast. Fleet's current location AT THE FIFTH SYSTEM has one small cyan triangle and single thin selection ring, labeled 'Current system'; no fictional interpolated mid-lane fleet position. Remaining eight legs are continuous crisp luminous cyan lines, with sparse small forward chevrons, NO DASHES on remaining active legs. Same distinctive destination marker. Completed and remaining sections meet clearly at current system. Keep intermediate stellar spheres unobscured.
Close views should show current-system neighborhood spanning about four hops and demonstrate that route overlay follows lane endpoints cleanly without swallowing stellar cores. Wide views show entire 12-hop route plus surrounding network; close views cropped to that same middle neighborhood, not entire route squashed. At wide scale reduce chevron density and omit intermediate text, keep route dashed versus solid distinction clearly apparent.
Readable style hierarchy: route and endpoint markers first, individual stellar cores second, ordinary lanes third, accepted background below. Maintain background art, palette and intensity; no new nebula directions. No lane traffic particles, animated trails, fabricated ETA/fuel numbers, territories, enemy coloring, large glowing ribbons, crossing labels or explanation paragraphs. Static visual concept only, doesn't establish planner/execution implementation.
