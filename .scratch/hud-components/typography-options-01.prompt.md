Typography directions:
A — CLEAN: neutral readable contemporary sans throughout; sentence-case headings, strong hierarchy through size/weight, quiet blue-gray labels, near-white names and figures. Broad accessible game UI feel.
B — TECHNICAL: compact crisp monospaced throughout; uppercase small section headings with modest tracking; near-white names and numbers with cyan labels; an organized instrument-panel character without tiny print.
C — HYBRID: slightly squared futuristic sans object names and section headings; highly readable normal sans body and labels; tabular monospaced numeric values. Small cyan heading accent, clear deliberate hierarchy.
D — COMMAND: substantial condensed sans object names and uppercase content section headings; normal humanist sans body; large strong numeric values; pale ice-blue heading emphasis. Stronger typographic personality, no exaggerated glow on body text.
Keep content positions, card sizes and frame identical so fonts, weights, cases, spacing and color hierarchy are the comparison. Four genuinely different but practical readable options, no fantasy alien glyphs, no illegible ultrathin text. Outside the cards add only clear comparison labels "A · CLEAN", "B · TECHNICAL", "C · HYBRID", "D · COMMAND". No overall title, no watermark.
