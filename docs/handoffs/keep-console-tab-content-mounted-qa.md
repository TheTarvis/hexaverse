J3 (leg 9, `:698-722`). The order anchor is weak, in both directions. `etaAt` is `commitAt` (taken after the countdown shows, so after the real commit) plus `durationMs / 5`, with a 1000 ms (`:705`) and 1500 ms (`:704`) fudge; `interiorAtEta` is "first interior response at or after eta minus 1 s", which can be an ordinary 20 s baseline-poll response, not the settling read, and the fallback `interiorBeforeReport` (`:708`) can precede the settle. `earlyCandidate` can be a running-status response in flight before the commit was processed (the capture starts at `seqBeforeCommit`, before `commitScan`), which carries no scan. Any pile request after such an early anchor passes (`:718`), so a build that re-reads the pile concurrently with the settling interior read (the wrong order the spec forbids) passes. Conversely a poll response that arrives after the settle's pile re-read becomes the anchor and gives a false FAILED. Exact ordering is owned by tier 1 (L:561 and L:593 hold the interior read and assert the pile is not read until it resolves); the journey is therefore only a liveness-on-the-wire witness, which is acceptable, but the name "after the settling read" overclaims. Close with: either relabel the assertion as liveness (a post-commit unfiltered request issued while the dock was collapsed, before any expansion, and at least one more than at the reload), or anchor on the last interior or no-scan response whose `at` precedes the first post-commit unfiltered request and require a gap-free sequence; do not fail when a later poll response exists.
