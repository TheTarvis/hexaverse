# add-burn-cost-overlay — architect's three notes on the round-1 repaired spec (2026-10-05)

From the architect's design-alignment hand-back, verbatim. Accepted by the
orchestrator for one bounded writer pass before adversary round 2.

- **Task 6.2, the UNTESTED threshold.** It reads "under 2 device pixels (a cost under about 5 t on a 200 t capacity)". The rail is 40 px for 200 t, so 5 t is one CSS pixel. `frontend/playwright.config.ts` sets no device scale factor, so 2 device pixels is 10 t there, not 5 t. I confirmed only that the config lacks the setting, not what a spec file might override. Design.md says 10 t, so it disagrees with that parenthetical on purpose; the delta's own "at least 2 device pixels long" is fine.
- **Delta 245-247, the fill's sample point.** "The centre pixel of the fill's part not covered by the extent" is not well defined on this geometry. With a 2 px centred band, the uncovered fill is a strip above, a strip below, and the full-height part past the band's end when the fill is longer. Its geometric centre can land on the band: an 18 px band in a 20 px fill puts it at about x = 10.4 on the middle row. Design.md gives a decidable reading (centre of the full-height part when the fill is longer, otherwise centre of the strip above the band). The delta should name the point that way, or the adversary can read the scenario as sampling the band twice.
- **Delta 47-50 and 159, "wholly inside the rail" and "no part of the mark lies outside the rail".** This is deliverable against the rail's box only. The rail is `rounded-full`, so at a cost of zero and at or above capacity the 3 px cap clips the mark's outer edge column and corners; the amber body is not clipped. No placement that puts the leading edge at the rail's start avoids this. Design.md states it under "Inside the rail is the rail's box". If the journey ever arranges the mark at a rail end, a literal pixel reading of line 159 would fail. Suggested: "no part of the mark lies past the rail's start or end".
