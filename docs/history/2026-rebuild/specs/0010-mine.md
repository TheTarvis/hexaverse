# 0010 — Mine (extraction verb)

> The slice's extraction action: a fleet parked at a minable body commits a
> mine run that resolves at its ETA into ore in the fleet's hold, drawing down
> the lode through #0005's event-sourced ledger — the **first mutating verb**
> on the action spine, and the spec that gives the fleet its cargo model.

## Problem / motivation

Mine is the last unspec'd verb of the first vertical slice (`../vision.md`
steps 4–5: *drill in → move → scan → mine*). Everything around it is already
in place: #0003 generates the bodies and their `resources [{kind, richness}]`
payloads, #0005 provides the async spine **and already sketches mine's delta
schema** (the `window`-capable ledger entry), #0007 carries the UI plumbing
(`useMine()`, select→preview→commit, the e2e that mines and returns after the
ETA), and #0009 explicitly delegates the starting hull's cargo/mining stats to
this spec (its "the verb specs equip it" line). What no spec defines is the
verb itself: what a run yields, where the ore lands, whether the lode
depletes, and what a fleet must be doing to mine.

It is also architecturally load-bearing beyond its size: mine is the
**reference implementation of #0005's resolution strategy 2** (mutating state
as an append-only ledger). #0005's acceptance tests literally name it ("scan
at T1 + mine at T2 resolved together at T3"). And it forces the **fleet cargo
model** into existence — the thing the whole later economy (hauling, trade,
piracy risk) hangs off.

## Goals

- A **mine action** on #0005's spine: commit (validate, freeze params, compute
  ETA, claim the single action slot) → resolve at ETA (append the lode delta +
  credit the fleet's hold in one transaction, exactly-once).
- A **fleet composition model** shaped for the endgame vision — ships as stat
  contributors, the fleet as the sole actor — even while the slice roster is
  one hull: per-ship stats aggregate to fleet totals (mining rate, hold
  capacity; later combat strength), fleet speed gates on the slowest hull.
- **Ore lands in the fleet's hold** — one fleet-level cargo pool, capacity =
  the sum of the roster's holds. No corporation stockpile, no teleporting to a
  bank: cargo being *on the fleet* is what makes hauling real later.
- **The lode depletes from day one** — `remaining(T)` is a fold over ledger
  deltas (#0005), so two fleets working one belt contest it honestly and
  "arrived to a picked-clean belt" works as decided.
- Yield is **clamped by reality at resolve**: lode remaining at `effectAt`
  (folded in ETA order) and free hold, with the shortfall reported in the
  action's `outcome`.
- **Equip the default hull**: the seed hull's `mineRate` and `cargoCap`
  live in this spec's params object (per #0009).

## Non-goals

- **Ship classes & building them** — the miner/cargo/fighter build tree is
  the build verb's spec (BACKLOG "Units / assets taxonomy", deliberately
  deferred to build-verb time). This spec ships the *shape* (roster +
  aggregation) with a single generalist `seed` class.
- **Cycle-quantized flow mining** — the slice mines point-at-ETA (#0005,
  decided); the `window {from, to, rate}` delta field is the no-migration
  hedge, written but unused.
- **Fuel–tonnage coupling** — "haul only what your fuel can push" is the
  deferred fuel economy (#0004 Decision 10; galaxy tier stays time-gated,
  #0008). Yield is recorded in tons, so mass-coupling later is a burn-cost
  modifier, not a schema change. Slice hauling is free; capacity is a flat cap.
- **Trade / selling the ore** — the goods market is deferred (⏸,
  `../plans/market.md`); the slice loop ends with a full hold.
- **Depletion-driven intel freshness** — "as-of T this belt held X" going
  stale *because* someone mined it is market-tier (#0006 → intel-market);
  the ledger this spec writes is exactly what that will read.
- **Refining, processing, resource sinks** — nothing consumes ore yet.
- **Mining structures** (rigs, sites, bases) — taxonomy/build-tree era.

## Proposed approach

Mine is a **mutating action** (#0005 strategy 2) committed by the fleet
against a minable feature and resolved by appending to that feature's ledger.

1. **Commit.** The player selects a minable feature their fleet is docked to
   (#0007's select→preview→commit), chooses what and how much to extract; the
   client previews `eta = tons ÷ fleetMineRate`. The server validates
   preconditions (docked to the feature, feature has that resource kind, tons
   ≤ free hold, single-slot free), freezes `{featureId, kind, tons, rate}`
   into `action.params`, computes the ETA, and commits through the action
   service — one transaction, per #0005.
2. **Between.** Nothing runs server-side; the owner's client animates
   progress from `(action, worldTime)` and self-schedules the ETA refetch
   (#0005 client rules; #0007 renders it).
3. **Resolve** (lazy on-read or the sweep, in ETA order). In one transaction:
   fold the feature's deltas ≤ `eta` to get `remaining`, clamp the take to
   `min(params.tons, remaining, freeHold)`, append the delta
   (`docId = actionId` — exactly-once), credit `fleet.cargo[kind]`, flip the
   action `done` with an honest `outcome` ("mined 40t metals" / "lode ran dry
   at 22t" / "hold full at 35t").

**Fleet model.** `fleet.ships[]` is a roster of `{class}` entries; classes map
to stats in the params object (`mineRate`, `cargoCap`, `speed`, …). Fleet
totals are derived: mining rate = Σ miners' rates, hold = Σ holds, speed =
slowest hull. The slice spawns `[{class: 'seed'}]` — one generalist hull with
modest everything (#0009's drive + scanner + cargo fit). Lore stays intact:
the fleet is one replicant mind (#0009); additional hulls are its unmanned
drones — stat contributors, never independent actors (#0004's
fleet-is-the-mover is untouched).

**Why docked, not co-hex:** bodies move (#0003 orbital elements) and a
coasting fleet rides *its own* orbit — co-hex is a moment, docked is a state.
#0004's dock-to-ride already keeps a docked fleet glued to its host through
orbital motion, so "docked to the target" is the one precondition that stays
true for the whole run with zero extra machinery, even when motion turns on.

## Design details

- **Where it lands:** `backend/mine/` — a thin verb service per #0005's
  dispatch pattern (handler→service; it commits through `backend/action/` and
  receives the resolve callback for `type: 'mine'`). Reads/writes fleets and
  features **through their services**, never DAOs (`backend/CLAUDE.md`).
- **Data model (additions):**

  ```
  fleets/{fleetId}                    # #0004's entity, gaining:
    ships   : [ { class: 'seed' | ... } ]     # roster; stats resolved via params
    cargo   : { metals: 12, volatiles: 3, ... }  # the one fleet-level pool (tons)

  features/{fid}/deltas/{actionId}    # exactly #0005's ledger — nothing new
    effectAt, amount (negative tons, per kind), window?, actorId
  ```

  `remaining(kind, T)` = the feature's `resources[kind]` initial richness-
  derived tonnage − fold of its deltas with `effectAt ≤ T`. **No materialized
  `remaining` field** (#0005 acceptance criterion). Hold capacity and mine
  rate are always derived from the roster — not stored on the fleet.
- **Params** (`MineParams`, alongside #0003's `GenerationParams` /​ #0004's
  movement params): per-class stats table (`seed: {mineRate, cargoCap,
  speed}`), richness→tonnage curve (how a `richness` value translates to a
  lode's starting tons), and a per-kind rate modifier if wanted. Frozen into
  the action at commit (#0005 — tuning changes affect only new commits).
- **Preconditions (validated in the commit transaction):** fleet is `docked`
  to the target feature (#0004 state machine); the feature's detail is rolled
  (guaranteed — you can't be docked in a system you haven't arrived in, and
  first arrival rolls detail, #0003); the feature carries the requested
  `kind`; requested tons ≤ current free hold; `activeActionId` free. **No
  scan required** — you may prospect by doing; you just commit blind to
  richness (the outcome tells you what you got). Scan-first stays the smart
  play, not a gate.
- **One kind per run.** A run targets a single resource kind chosen at
  commit (frozen in params). Multi-kind bodies take multiple runs — keeps the
  delta, the clamp, and the picker trivially simple; a "mine everything"
  convenience can layer on later without schema change.
- **Contested lodes are optimistic** (#0005, decided): no reservations; two
  fleets' runs apply in ETA order at resolve, the later one clamps against
  what's left. On-theme for the frontier.
- **API (sketch):**
  - `POST /v1/fleets/{fleetId}/mine` `{featureId, kind, tons}` → commits via
    the action service, returns the action (ETA included). Cancel/acknowledge
    ride #0005's generic endpoints.
  - Fleet reads (existing #0004 surface) now include `ships`, `cargo`, and
    derived capacities. Board reads (#0003/#0007) already return features;
    lode remaining surfaces through the fog rules — your own presence sees it
    live, others see their `asOf` observation (#0006).
- **Events:** #0005's `ActionCompleted` envelope on resolve → SSE; **no new
  Pub/Sub constants or channels.**
- **Frontend** (all inside #0007's remit — listed for wiring, not redesigned
  here): `useMine()` mutation → mine service → `apiClient`; select a docked-at
  feature → facts panel offers Mine with a kind picker + tonnage input
  clamped to `min(freeHold, lode-as-known)`, live ETA preview; commit clears
  selection and arms the ETA refetch. Hold contents + capacity join the
  fleet readout. `data-testid` per the `/check` validators.
- **Testing:** emulator tests (`backend/mine/`): commit validation (each
  precondition), exactly-once resolve under concurrent resolvers, the
  ETA-order fold shared with #0005's criterion (scan at T1 + mine at T2
  resolved together at T3), both clamps (dry lode, full hold) with honest
  `outcome`s, aggregation math over multi-ship rosters (unit test — the slice
  spawns one). Vitest for hook/service; the slice e2e lives in #0007.

## Decisions (2026-07-09, from the fleet-composition discussion)

1. **Ships compose, the fleet acts** (*Travis*). The endgame picture — e.g.
   3 miners + 1 cargo + 2 fighters — is one fleet whose roster contributes
   stats (rate, hold, speed-gates-on-slowest, later combat). Ship classes are
   never independent actors; #0004/#0008/#0009's fleet-is-the-mover stands.
   The build tree that makes rosters grow is the build verb's spec.
2. **Ore lives on the fleet** (*Travis*) — one fleet-level cargo pool,
   capacity = Σ roster holds. Per-ship allocation is bookkeeping with no
   gameplay until ships can detach (replication/build era); the pool is
   derived-capacity now, splittable later.
3. **Fuel–tonnage stays deferred** (*Travis' "haul what your fuel can push"*,
   deliberately not pulled forward) — it is the #0004 fuel economy. Tons are
   in the ledger from day one, so it lands later as a burn-cost modifier.
4. **Docked is the mining precondition** (*dev*) — the one state that
   survives orbital motion; co-hex is a coincidence, docked is a commitment.
5. **Point-at-ETA, window-hedged** — inherited from #0005 (already decided
   there; restated for locality).
6. **Runs are sized by tonnage target** (*Travis, 2026-07-09*) — "mine 40t"
   → ETA = tons ÷ rate. The delta amount and hold clamp fall straight out,
   and "fill the hold" is the natural picker default. (Duration-picked runs
   make the *amount* the derived thing, which fights the hold clamp.)
7. **Lodes visibly deplete in the slice** (*Travis, 2026-07-09*) —
   emphatically yes: **depletion keeps players exploring and moving** — a
   worked-out home system is the push to the next one, and it seeds
   depletion-with-a-story / intel spoilage later (`../plans/hex-board.md`).
   The ledger fold makes it nearly free.

## Open questions

*None blocking* — the two accept-gating calls (run sizing, slice depletion)
resolved 2026-07-09 as Decisions 6–7.

- **Starting numbers** (seed hull `mineRate`/`cargoCap`, richness→tonnage
  curve) — tuning-time, in `MineParams` with spike-informed defaults; not a
  blocker. **Owner: dev, at implementation.**
- **Belt coasting as "docked"** — does coasting *inside* a belt arc count as
  docked to the belt? Nice texture, zero slice value (dock to the belt
  feature works today). **Deferred** to when orbital motion turns on.
