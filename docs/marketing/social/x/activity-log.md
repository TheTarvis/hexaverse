## Learnings

**The comp makes people act; the personal angle makes people look.** The two
posts that named **EVE-lite** got all the engagement — likes, the reply, the
repost. The one that didn't (the "Side Project" joke) got the most impressions
and zero engagement. "EVE-lite web based MMO" is a complete pitch in five
words, cheap to react to without watching anything.

**Asking for a verdict works.** Both engaged posts posed a question — "Yay/Nay"
and "Hows this feel" — rather than announcing. The 07-07 post had the *lowest*
reach but earned the only repost, so its action-per-impression beat both.

**None of them were "here's what I built today."** All three either asked for a
reaction or made a joke. Keep it that way.

**Caveat:** three posts and single-digit engagement. These are hypotheses to
test, not rules.
